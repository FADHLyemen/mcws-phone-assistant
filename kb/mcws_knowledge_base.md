## About MCWS
MCWS (Muslim Community of Western Suburbs) is a non-profit masjid serving the western suburbs of Detroit, Michigan. It was established in **1977** by young Muslim parents to preserve Islamic identity and build a place of worship, learning, and belonging. It has grown into a full community resource offering daily prayers, seminars, study circles, youth programs, and Islamic education.

Mission: create a peaceful, caring environment where individuals grow closer to God and become positive contributors to their families and the wider community. All activities are open to the public. Values: community service, justice & compassion, inclusivity & unity, and prayer for peace.

## Prayer & Iqama Times
Five daily congregational prayers are held at the masjid. Daily adhan (begins) and iqama (jama'ah) times, sunrise, and Jumu'ah times are available LIVE — always answer time questions using the get_prayer_times tool, never a memorized value.
- **Jumu'ah (Friday):** The masjid's published Friday schedule lists two times at each campus — **MCWS Masjid: 12:30 PM and 1:45 PM**; **Medina Activity Center (MAC): 2:15 PM and 3:30 PM**. (The source lists these under "Begins" and "Jama'ah" labels; do not assert whether they are separate Jumu'ah services or khutbah-start vs. prayer times — if the caller needs certainty about which time to attend, offer to confirm or refer them to mcws.org.) The get_prayer_times tool returns the current MCWS Jumu'ah times live.
- Maghrib iqama is at sunset.
